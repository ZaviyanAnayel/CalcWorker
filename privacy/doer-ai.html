Privacy Policy — Doer AI

Effective: September 25, 2026 · Developer: Zaviyan (Zaviyan LLC)

1. What Doer AI does

Doer AI turns your voice or typed commands into concrete phone actions (call, text, alarm, navigation, email), always asking for your confirmation first.

2. Data we collect directly

Doer AI does not require an account, does not run its own servers that store your data, and does not collect your name, email, phone number, or precise location.

3. On-device processing

Commands are interpreted on your device. Voice audio is processed by your phone's own speech services; we operate no voice servers and store no recordings.

4. Optional AI key

Doer AI works without any API key using its built-in offline engine. If you choose to enter your own AI API key for smarter answers, the key is stored only on your device and your questions are sent directly from your device to the AI provider you chose. We never see or store your key or your questions.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Doer AI is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers such as the Google Advertising ID, coarse device/app information, and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads where you have consented. This data is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android Settings → Privacy → Ads, and you can change consent anytime from the app's privacy choices screen.

6. Children

Doer AI is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Data retention & deletion

Because we operate no servers that store your personal data, there is nothing on our side to delete. Advertising data held by Google can be managed via your Google ad settings linked above.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change and continued use of the app means you accept the update.
