Understanding the 2026 Etsy Fee Structure

Etsy provides a global marketplace for millions of independent artisans, vintage curators, and small craft businesses. However, calculating true profit on Etsy can be deceptive because fees are layered across multiple categories. Without understanding exactly what Etsy charges on every sale, sellers risk underpricing their inventory and operating at razor-thin or negative margins.

Detailed Breakdown of Etsy Seller Fees

  1. Listing Fee ($0.20 USD): Charged each time you list an item or renew an existing listing. Listings expire after 4 months if unsold. If an item sells multiple quantities in one order, an additional $0.20 auto-renewal fee applies to each additional unit sold.
  2. Transaction Fee (6.5%): Etsy takes a mandatory 6.5% cut of the total sale price, including any shipping fees charged to the customer and gift-wrapping options.
  3. Etsy Payments Processing Fee (3% + $0.25): In the United States, transactions processed through Etsy Payments incur a 3% merchant processing fee plus a flat $0.25 transaction surcharge.
  4. Offsite Ads Fee (12% to 15%): Etsy advertises products across Google, Instagram, Pinterest, and Bing. If a shopper clicks an offsite ad and makes a purchase from your shop within 30 days, Etsy charges an advertising fee. For shops that made under $10,000 in the past 12 months, the fee is 15% (optional). For shops exceeding $10,000 in trailing 12-month revenue, the fee drops to 12% and participation becomes mandatory.

The Mathematical Net Profit Formula

Gross Order Value = Item Sale Price + Shipping Charged to Buyer

Total Etsy Fees = $0.20 + (Gross Order × 0.065) + (Gross Order × 0.03 + $0.25) + (Gross Order × Offsite Ads Rate)

Net Profit = Gross Order Value - Total Etsy Fees - Product Material Cost - Shipping Paid

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Why does Etsy charge fees on shipping amounts?

In the past, some online merchants would price an item at $1.00 and charge $40.00 for shipping to bypass platform percentage fees. Etsy and other major marketplaces apply transaction fees to shipping charges to eliminate fee avoidance and promote fair pricing transparency.

Should I offer "Free Shipping" on Etsy?

Etsy's search algorithm prioritizes listings that offer free shipping to US buyers on orders over $35. Most successful sellers bake their average shipping cost directly into the retail item price (e.g., pricing at $50 with free shipping instead of $45 + $5 shipping).

Do Etsy sellers need to pay self-employment tax?

Yes. Profit generated from your Etsy shop is classified as self-employment business income by the IRS. You must report net profits on Schedule C of Form 1040 and pay standard self-employment tax (15.3%) if your annual net earnings exceed $400.

Strategic Practical Questions

Can I opt out of Etsy Offsite Ads?

If your shop has generated less than $10,000 in gross sales over the prior 365 consecutive days, you can voluntarily opt out of Offsite Ads in your shop manager dashboard. Once your shop generates $10,000 or more in trailing 12-month revenue, participation is legally mandatory for the lifetime of your shop.

What is the Etsy Listing Renewal fee?

Every active listing on Etsy expires after 4 months unless sold. When an item sells, Etsy automatically renews the listing for another 4-month cycle and charges a $0.20 renewal fee. If a listing contains 10 units in stock, you pay $0.20 upfront, and another $0.20 each time a unit sells.
