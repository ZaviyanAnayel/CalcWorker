Frequently Asked Questions (TikTok Coins & Gifts)

How much is 1 TikTok coin worth in USD?

When purchased on the official TikTok desktop website (tiktok.com/coin), 1 TikTok coin costs approximately $0.0106 USD (about 1 cent). When bought in the mobile app via Apple App Store or Google Play, 1 coin costs roughly $0.014 to $0.015 USD due to app store platform processing fees.

How much does a creator get from a TikTok gift?

When a viewer sends a TikTok gift, the creator receives 1 Diamond for every 1 Coin value of the gift. TikTok retains roughly a 50% commission. Each Diamond is worth exactly $0.005 USD ($0.01 divided by 2). Therefore, if a gift costs 1,000 coins (approx. $10.60 to buy), the creator receives 1,000 Diamonds, which cashes out to exactly $5.00 USD before payment processing fees.

How much is the TikTok Lion gift worth in real money?

The TikTok Lion gift costs 29,999 coins. To purchase the Lion on the TikTok web store costs approximately $318 USD (or up to $420+ through in-app mobile purchase). When received by a creator, it generates 29,999 Diamonds, which cashes out to exactly $149.99 USD in real creator income.

How much is the TikTok Universe gift worth in USD?

The TikTok Universe is one of the most prestigious gifts on the platform, costing either 34,999 or 44,999 coins depending on the version. At 44,999 coins, a viewer pays approximately $477 USD on Web (and over $600 in-app). The creator receives 44,999 Diamonds, netting a cashout value of $224.99 USD.

Why are TikTok coins cheaper on the website than on the mobile app?

Apple (App Store) and Google (Google Play) charge a standard 30% in-app purchase fee on digital goods. When you buy coins through the TikTok smartphone app, TikTok passes this fee on to users. Buying directly via desktop web browser on tiktok.com/coin bypasses mobile app stores, saving you up to 31% on coin purchases.

What are TikTok Diamonds and how do you cash them out?

Diamonds are virtual measurement points that TikTok awards creators when they receive gifts on Live streams. Once you accumulate at least $100 USD worth of Diamonds (20,000 Diamonds), you can cash them out to your connected PayPal account, Zelle, or direct bank transfer from your TikTok creator balance.

What is the minimum and maximum amount you can withdraw from TikTok Live gifts?

In the United States and most countries, the minimum withdrawal threshold is $100 USD (20,000 Diamonds). The maximum daily withdrawal limit is typically $1,000 USD per day for standard accounts, though verified high-volume creators can request elevated daily withdrawal limits from TikTok support.

Do TikTok creators pay taxes on Live stream gifts?

Yes. The IRS and international tax agencies classify TikTok Diamond cashouts as taxable self-employment income, not tax-free personal gifts. If you cash out more than the federal reporting threshold, TikTok or PayPal will issue an IRS Form 1099-NEC or 1099-K.
