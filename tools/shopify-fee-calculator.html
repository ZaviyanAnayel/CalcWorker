Frequently Asked Questions (Shopify Fees & Profit)

How much does Shopify charge per transaction in 2026?

If you use Shopify Payments, online credit card rates are: Basic plan (2.9% + $0.30), 'Shopify' plan (2.7% + $0.30), and Advanced plan (2.5% + $0.30). If you use an external third-party payment gateway (like PayPal or Stripe directly), Shopify imposes an additional transaction fee of 2.0% (Basic), 1.0% (Shopify), or 0.5% (Advanced).

How much do Shopify subscription plans cost?

When billed monthly, the plans are: Basic Shopify ($39/mo), Shopify ($105/mo), and Advanced Shopify ($399/mo). If paid annually upfront, Shopify typically discounts subscription pricing by 25%.

When should you upgrade from Basic Shopify to the Shopify plan?

You should upgrade to the $105/mo Shopify plan when your monthly store revenue exceeds approximately $33,000. At this volume, the 0.2% savings on credit card processing (2.7% vs 2.9%) completely offsets the $66/month increase in subscription fees.

Is Shopify cheaper than Amazon or Etsy?

On a per-sale basis, yes. Shopify's transaction take-rate is only around 2.9% + 30¢, compared to 9.5%–10% on Etsy and 15%–40% on Amazon. However, with Shopify you must fund your own customer acquisition (paid ads or SEO), whereas Amazon and Etsy provide existing marketplace buyer traffic.

What is a good profit margin for a Shopify store?

Healthy direct-to-consumer (DTC) e-commerce stores aim for a 15% to 25% net profit margin after COGS, ad spend (CAC), shipping, Shopify subscription fees, and app expenses.

What hidden fees exist on Shopify?

Common secondary expenses include paid Shopify App Store plugins ($20 to $200+/month for email marketing, upsells, reviews), custom domain renewal ($14/year), chargeback dispute fees ($15 per occurrence), and paid advertising costs.

How is Break-Even CAC calculated for Shopify?

Break-Even Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) is the maximum amount you can spend to acquire an order without losing money: Retail Price minus (COGS + Shipping Cost + Shopify Payment Fees). If your product sells for $50 and production/fees equal $22, your break-even CAC is $28.

Does Shopify take a cut of shipping fees charged to the buyer?

Shopify does not charge a platform commission on shipping, but credit card processing fees (e.g. 2.9% + 30¢) apply to the total transaction amount paid by the customer, which includes shipping and taxes.

Strategic Practical Questions

Can I avoid the Shopify 2% transaction fee?

Yes. By activating Shopify Payments as your primary checkout payment gateway, Shopify completely waives all third-party transaction penalty fees. You only pay standard wholesale credit card processing fees.

What is Shopify Plus and when should a merchant upgrade?

Shopify Plus is the enterprise tier starting at $2,300 per month, designed for high-growth brands generating over $1,000,000 to $2,000,000 in annual revenue. It offers custom checkout scripting, wholesale B2B portals, dedicated account management, and negotiable card processing rates as low as 2.15% + 30¢.
