📐 How It Works: Mathematical Formulas & Methodology

Federal Truth in Lending Act (TILA) Payday APR Formula Verified Calculation Model
Core Formula:
\text{APR} = \left( \frac{\text{Finance Charge}}{\text{Principal Loan Amount}} \right) \times \left( \frac{365}{\text{Loan Term in Days}} \right) \times 100\%
In Plain English: Annualizes short-term loan fees across 365 calendar days in accordance with Federal Reserve Regulation Z disclosure mandates.
Mathematical Variables & Inputs:
  • Finance Charge: Flat fee charged per $100 borrowed (typically $15 to $20 per $100 for a two-week period).
  • Loan Term: Contractual repayment window until next payroll disbursement (typically 14 days).
  • Compounded Rollover: Refinancing the loan multiplies fees without reducing original borrowed principal.

📝 Step-by-Step Practical Calculation Example

Follow this real-world example to calculate or verify your numbers manually:

  2. If the borrower rolls the loan over 3 times (6 weeks total), they pay $225 in finance charges while still owing the full $500 principal.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is the APR on payday loans so staggeringly high?

Payday lenders disguise triple-digit interest rates as modest 'flat fees' (e.g. $15 per $100 borrowed). However, because that $15 fee is charged for only 14 days, annualizing that rate yields a staggering 391% APR.

What is the payday loan 'rollover debt trap'?

When a borrower cannot repay the full loan plus fee on payday, they pay another fee to renew or 'roll over' the loan for another two weeks. According to the CFPB, over 80% of payday loans are rolled over within 30 days.

What are Payday Alternative Loans (PALs)?

Federal credit unions offer Payday Alternative Loans (PALs) with loan amounts up to $2,000, repayment terms from 1 to 12 months, and interest rates legally capped at 28% APR.

Does the Military Lending Act (MLA) protect service members from payday loans?

Yes. The federal Military Lending Act caps interest rates and fees on consumer credit for active-duty service members and their dependents at a strict maximum of 36% Military Annual Percentage Rate (MAPR).

⚠️ Payday Loan Real APR Economics & Statutory Usury Protections

Payday loans are short-term, high-cost consumer advances typically ranging from $100 to $1,000 designed to be repaid on the borrower's next paycheck.
