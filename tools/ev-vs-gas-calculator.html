📐 How It Works: Mathematical Formulas & Methodology

Total Cost of Ownership Break-Even Formulation Verified Calculation Model
Core Formula:
\text{Annual Fuel Savings} = \text{Miles} \times \left( \frac{P_{\text{gas}}}{\text{MPG}} - \frac{P_{\text{electric}}}{\text{Efficiency}_{\text{mi/kWh}}} \right)
In Plain English: Calculates the total driving mileage required for cumulative fuel and maintenance savings to offset the initial purchase price differential.
Mathematical Variables & Inputs:
  • P_{gas}: Retail price per gallon of unleaded gasoline ($/gallon).
  • P_{electric}: Residential home charging electricity rate ($/kWh).
  • Efficiency: EV driving economy (typically 3.0 to 4.2 miles per kWh).
  • Payback: Vehicle price premium minus available federal Clean Vehicle Credits (IRC § 30D) divided by annual operational savings.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I compare electric vehicle charging costs against gasoline prices?

Compare the cost per mile for each powertrain: Gas Cost per Mile = Gas Price per Gallon ÷ Vehicle MPG. EV Cost per Mile = Electricity Rate per kWh ÷ EV Efficiency (miles/kWh). For example, with gas at $3.60/gal and a 30 MPG car ($0.12/mile), an EV getting 3.5 mi/kWh charging at $0.14/kWh costs $0.04/mile—a 66.7% operational fuel discount.

How much does it cost to charge an electric car at home?

Charging an average 75 kWh EV battery from 10% to 80% requires approximately 52.5 kWh. At the US average residential electricity rate of $0.16 per kWh, a full home charging session costs approximately $8.40, providing roughly 210 to 250 miles of driving range.

What is the maintenance cost advantage of an EV over an internal combustion engine?

EVs have approximately 20 moving drivetrain parts compared to over 2,000 in a traditional gasoline engine. EVs eliminate engine oil changes, spark plugs, timing belts, fuel injectors, catalytic converters, and oxygen sensors. Regenerative braking also extends brake pad life to over 100,000 miles, reducing annual maintenance expenses by 40% to 50%.

How does cold winter weather affect EV battery range?

In sub-freezing temperatures (below 32°F / 0°C), EV driving range can decrease by 20% to 35%. This reduction is driven by chemical lithium-ion battery resistance and the energy required by cabin heating systems (resistive heaters or heat pumps) drawn directly from the traction battery pack.

⚡ Electric Vehicle vs. Internal Combustion: 5-Year Total Cost of Ownership

Comparing an Electric Vehicle (EV) to an Internal Combustion Engine (ICE) vehicle requires analyzing upfront purchase premiums, federal clean vehicle tax credits under IRC § 30D, residential kilowatt-hour electricity rates versus volatile retail gasoline, and long-term powertrain maintenance differentials.

While electric vehicles often carry higher upfront MSRPs and slightly elevated insurance premiums due to specialized repair networks, they eliminate engine oil changes, transmission flushes, spark plug replacements, and catalytic converter theft risks, while delivering 60% to 75% savings in fuel costs per mile driven.
