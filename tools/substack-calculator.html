📐 How It Works: Mathematical Formulas & Methodology

Substack Creator Net Take-Home Revenue Model Verified Calculation Model
Core Formula:
\text{Net MRR} = \sum(\text{Subscribers} \times \text{Price}) \times (1 - 0.10) - (\text{Gross Revenue} \times 0.029 + \text{Subscribers} \times \$0.30)
In Plain English: Calculates gross monthly recurring revenue and subtracts Substack's 10% platform share and Stripe's 2.9% + $0.30 payment processing charges.
Mathematical Variables & Inputs:
  • Subscribers: Active recurring paying subscribers on monthly or annual plans.
  • Price: Contractual subscription fee (standard $5-$15/month).
  • Substack Fee: 10.0% statutory platform commission retained by Substack Inc.
  • Stripe Fee: Credit card processing charge of 2.9% plus $0.30 per successful billing transaction.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I export my subscriber list if I leave Substack?

Yes. Unlike social media algorithms (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok), on Substack you 100% own your email list and your Stripe payment relationships. You can export your full subscriber CSV list and migrate credit card billing to another platform like Beehiiv or Ghost with zero disruption.

What should I charge for my paid newsletter?

For consumer lifestyle and culture newsletters, the standard pricing is $5 to $8/month (or $50 to $80/year). For professional B2B, finance, crypto, or career advancement publications, price at $15 to $50/month (or $150 to $500/year), as readers often expense the cost through their employer's education budget.
