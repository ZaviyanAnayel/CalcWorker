In Plain English: Calculates 40% federal estate tax on net estate value after deducting unlimited marital transfers, charitable bequests, and the lifetime unified exclusion.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the federal estate tax exemption in 2026?

The federal unified gift and estate tax exemption is over $13.6 million for single individuals and over $27.2 million for married couples.

What is portability of the estate tax exemption?

Portability allows a surviving spouse to elect to use the deceased spouse's unused exclusion (DSUE) by filing timely IRS Form 706.

What is the annual gift tax exclusion?

In 2026, you can gift up to $18,000 per recipient ($36,000 for married couples splitting gifts) per year without filing a gift tax return or using lifetime exemption.

Do states have separate estate or inheritance taxes?

Yes. About 12 US states and DC impose separate state estate taxes with lower exemptions (e.g., $1M to $7M), and 6 states impose inheritance taxes based on who receives the property.

🏛️ Federal Estate & Gift Tax Architecture: Unified Lifetime Exemption Modeling

The federal estate tax is a transfer tax levied on the net fair market value of a deceased individual's worldwide assets before distribution to beneficiaries. Under current federal tax statutes, the unified lifetime gift and estate tax exemption shields substantial wealth from the top 40% federal estate tax bracket.

Estate planning requires tracking both lifetime taxable gifts (using IRS Form 709) and the deceased gross estate (IRS Form 706). Married couples can leverage 'Portability' (the Deceased Spousal Unused Exclusion or DSUE) to combine their individual exemptions into a joint transfer shield.

⚡ High-Net-Worth Estate Tax Shield Strategies

  • File Form 706 for Portability Election: Even if a deceased spouse's estate owes zero tax, executors must file a timely Form 706 to elect portability, transferring unused exclusion to the surviving spouse.
  • Utilize Irrevocable Life Insurance Trusts (ILIT): Holding substantial life insurance policies inside an ILIT removes death benefit proceeds from the gross estate, preventing 40% estate tax erosion.
  • Leverage Annual Exclusion Gifts Systematically: Gifting $19,000 per year to children, grandchildren, and their spouses systematically trims estate valuation without consuming any lifetime exemption.
