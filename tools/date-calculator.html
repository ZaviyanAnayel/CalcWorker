❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the date 30 days from today?

Use the quick +30 Days button above to view the exact calendar date, day of the week, and total business day count 30 days from right now.

How do business days differ from calendar days?

Calendar days include all 7 days of the week. Business days exclude Saturdays and Sundays (standard 5-day working week), essential for legal and financial delivery windows.

How many days are in a leap year?

A standard Gregorian year has 365 days, while a leap year contains 366 days, adding an intercalary leap day on February 29th to align the calendar with Earth’s solar orbit.

📅 Gregorian Calendar Mathematics & Business Day Calculations

Modern date arithmetic conforms to the Gregorian calendar (introduced in 1582 by Pope Gregory XIII). Accurately projecting deadlines and contract milestones requires strict adherence to mathematical rules:

  • The 400-Year Leap Century Rule: A year is a leap year if divisible by 4, except for end-of-century years which must be divisible by 400. Thus, 2000 was a leap year, but 1900 was not, and 2100 will not be.
  • Business vs. Calendar Days: Commercial contracts and court filing deadlines typically specify business working days (Monday through Friday, excluding official federal holidays such as New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Thanksgiving, and Christmas).
  • Statutory Deadlines: The IRS tax filing deadline (typically April 15) automatically rolls forward to the following business day if it falls on a Saturday, Sunday, or Emancipation Day in Washington D.C.
