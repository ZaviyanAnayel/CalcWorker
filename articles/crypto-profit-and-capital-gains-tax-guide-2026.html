Foundations: Tax Basics & Core Math

When you trade, stake, or earn cryptocurrency, the IRS treats each transaction as a taxable event. The profit (or loss) you realize is the difference between the amount you receive and your cost basis. Understanding the math early prevents costly surprises at tax time.

Gain = Sale Price – Cost Basis

For a portfolio of multiple trades, the net profit is the sum of individual gains. To express performance as a percentage, use the Return on Investment (ROI) formula:

ROI = (Net Profit ÷ Total Invested) × 100%

Both formulas work regardless of the accounting method you choose (FIFO, HIFO, or LIFO). The key is to keep precise records of dates, amounts, and fair market values (FMV) at the moment of each transaction.

Step‑by‑Step 2026 Example Calculation

Scenario: Jane bought Bitcoin (BTC) on three separate dates and sold part of her holdings in December 2026. She also incurred a wash‑sale situation with Ethereum (ETH).

Purchase History

  • Jan 15 2023: 2.0 BTC @ $22,000 each (Total $44,000)
  • Jun 10 2024: 1.5 BTC @ $31,000 each (Total $46,500)
  • Mar 05 2025: 0.8 BTC @ $38,500 each (Total $30,800)

Sale Transaction (Dec 20 2026)

  • Sold 1.5 BTC @ $38,000 each (Total $57,000)

Cost‑Basis Methods

  1. FIFO (First‑In‑First‑Out): Use earliest purchases first.
    Cost Basis = (2.0 BTC × $22,000) = $44,000 → 1.5 BTC taken from this lot = $33,000
    Gain = $57,000 – $33,000 = $24,000 (short‑term because holding < 1 yr?) Actually holding > 3 yr, so long‑term.
  2. HIFO (Highest‑In‑First‑Out): Use the highest cost first.
    Cost Basis = (0.8 BTC × $38,500) + (0.7 BTC × $31,000) = $30,800 + $21,700 = $52,500
    Gain = $57,000 – $52,500 = $4,500 (long‑term).
  3. LIFO (Last‑In‑First‑Out): Use most recent purchases.
    Cost Basis = (0.8 BTC × $38,500) + (0.7 BTC × $31,000) = $52,500 (same as HIFO for this quantity)
    Gain = $4,500 (long‑term).

Wash‑Sale Example (ETH)

  • Feb 01 2026: Sold 10 ETH @ $2,200 each → $22,000 loss of $5,000 (cost $27,000).
  • Feb 15 2026: Repurchased 10 ETH @ $2,250 each.

Because the repurchase occurred within 30 days, the $5,000 loss is disallowed and added to the new basis: $27,000 + $5,000 = $32,000. This higher basis will reduce future gains.

Net ROI Calculation (using HIFO)

Net Profit = $57,000 – $52,500 = $4,500
Total Invested (HIFO) = $52,500
ROI = ($4,500 ÷ $52,500) × 100 = 8.57%

Applying the 2026 long‑term federal rate of 15% and an average state rate of 5% yields a tax of $4,500 × 20% = $900, leaving a take‑home profit of $3,600.

Optimization Strategies for Crypto Traders

Implementing tax‑aware practices can dramatically improve your after‑tax returns. Below are proven tactics you can apply today.

  1. Adopt HIFO when you expect a market rally; it locks in the highest cost basis and minimizes taxable gains.
  2. Use a dedicated crypto tax software that integrates with exchanges and automatically flags wash‑sale events.
  3. Consider tax‑loss harvesting before year‑end: sell under‑performing assets to realize losses that offset gains.
  4. Hold assets for > 1 year to qualify for long‑term rates; the 2026 long‑term top rate (20% federal) is still lower than the highest ordinary income bracket (37%).
