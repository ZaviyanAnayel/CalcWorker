Foundations: How Crypto Gains Are Taxed & Core Math

In the United States, every cryptocurrency transaction that results in a profit is a taxable event. The IRS treats crypto as property, so capital gains rules apply. The key distinction is whether the holding period is short-term (≤ 12 months) or long-term (> 12 months). Short‑term gains are taxed at ordinary‑income rates, while long‑term gains enjoy preferential rates.

The fundamental calculation is simple but must be applied consistently across all trades:

Gain = Sale Price – Cost Basis

Where Cost Basis depends on the accounting method you choose (FIFO, LIFO, HIFO). Accurate record‑keeping is essential because the IRS can audit any discrepancy.

Step‑by‑Step 2026 Example: Calculating Net ROI

Jane Doe bought and sold Bitcoin in 2026. Below is a realistic timeline:

  • Jan 15: Bought 0.5 BTC @ $30,000 each → $15,000 (Lot A)
  • Mar 10: Bought 0.3 BTC @ $32,000 each → $9,600 (Lot B)
  • Jun 05: Sold 0.4 BTC for $35,000 each → $14,000
  • Oct 20: Sold 0.2 BTC for $28,000 each → $5,600

Assume Jane’s ordinary‑income tax bracket is 24% and her long‑term rate is 15% (income $120,000). She also pays the 3.8% NIIT on net investment income.

Step 1 – Choose Cost Basis (FIFO)

FIFO Cost for Jun 05 sale = (0.4 BTC from Lot A) → 0.4 × $30,000 = $12,000

Gain = $14,000 – $12,000 = $2,000 (short‑term)

Step 2 – Remaining Basis after Jun 05

Lot A remaining = 0.1 BTC @ $30,000 = $3,000 Lot B unchanged = 0.3 BTC @ $32,000 = $9,600

Step 3 – Cost Basis for Oct 20 sale (HIFO)

HIFO picks highest cost first → use 0.2 BTC from Lot B @ $32,000 = $6,400

Gain = $5,600 – $6,400 = –$800 (short‑term loss)

Step 4 – Apply Wash‑Sale Rule

Jane repurchased 0.2 BTC on Nov 01 at $29,000. Because the loss occurred within 30 days, the $800 loss is disallowed and added to the new lot’s basis:

New Lot basis = $5,800 (purchase $5,800 + disallowed $800)

Step 5 – Tax Liability

Tax on $2,000 gain = $2,000 × 24% = $480 Net Investment Tax = $2,000 × 3.8% = $76 Total Tax = $556

Step 6 – Net ROI

Total Invested = $15,000 + $9,600 + $5,800 = $30,400 Total Received = $14,000 + $5,600 = $19,600 Net Cash Flow = $19,600 – $30,400 = –$10,800 After‑Tax Cash Flow = –$10,800 – $556 = –$11,356 ROI = (After‑Tax Cash Flow ÷ Total Invested) × 100 = –37.4%

Jane ends the year with a –37.4% after‑tax ROI, illustrating how wash‑sale rules and cost‑basis choices dramatically affect outcomes.

Optimization Strategies to Maximize After‑Tax Returns

Below are proven tactics you can implement today:

  1. Harvest Long‑Term Gains – Hold positions > 12 months to qualify for the 0%/15%/20% rates.
  2. Strategic Cost‑Basis Selection – Use HIFO when you expect a market dip; it locks in higher basis, reducing taxable gains.
  3. Timing Wash‑Sales – If you must realize a loss, wait > 30 days before repurchasing the same coin, or buy a correlated alt‑coin instead.
  4. Utilize Tax‑Advantaged Accounts – Some crypto can be held in self‑directed IRAs, deferring or eliminating capital gains.
  5. Quarterly Estimated Payments – Avoid penalties by projecting short‑term gains and paying quarterly estimates based on your projected ordinary‑income bracket.
  6. Leverage the Crypto Profit & ROI Calculator – Run multiple scenarios (FIFO vs HIFO) to see the tax impact before executing trades.

Implementing even two of these strategies can shave several percentage points off your effective tax rate, boosting your net ROI.
